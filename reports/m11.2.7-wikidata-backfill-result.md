## Approach

1. **Detect gap**: `SELECT c.wiki_data_id FROM cities c LEFT JOIN wikidata_staging w ON w.qid = c.wiki_data_id WHERE c.wiki_data_id IS NOT NULL AND c.wiki_data_id != '' AND w.qid IS NULL`
2. **Query SPARQL**: 3 batches of 1,000 Q-ids each
3. **Insert with `INSERT OR REPLACE`**: not `ON CONFLICT` (qid is not the primary key — staging_id is)
4. **Batched inserts**: chunks of 11 rows (7 cols × 11 = 77 vars, under D1's 100-var limit)

## Gotchas hit

- **`ON CONFLICT` doesn't work** — `qid` is not a unique constraint, only `staging_id` (PRIMARY KEY) is
  - Workaround: use `INSERT OR REPLACE` (matches M11.2's original strategy)
- **1000-row batch fails** — 7 cols × 1000 = 7000 vars, way over D1's 100-var limit per prepared statement
  - Workaround: split into chunks of 11 rows (77 vars)
- **HTTPError 400 vs other errors**: silent failures; need explicit error handling in `http_query()`
