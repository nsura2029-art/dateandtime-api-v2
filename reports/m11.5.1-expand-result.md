## Performance optimization

Combined the 3 ACS queries (Sex by Age, Income, Education) into a single 3-way LEFT JOIN:

```sql
SELECT a.*, i.median_income, e.*
FROM us_acs_attributes a
LEFT JOIN us_acs_income_attributes i ON i.fips_geoid = a.fips_geoid
LEFT JOIN us_acs_education_attributes e ON e.fips_geoid = a.fips_geoid
WHERE a.city_id = ? LIMIT 1
```

**Result**: US city detail endpoint is now ~600-900ms (was ~1500-2100ms before M11.5.1 expand).

## Gotchas hit

- **B15003 variable mapping**: I initially had the wrong mapping. The 25 variables are:
  - E001: Total 25+
  - **E002-E016**: less than HS (15 variables for grades K-12 without diploma) — I had it as E002-E010 (only 9 variables, missing grades 5-8)
  - E017: regular HS diploma
  - E018: GED
  - E019-E020: some college, no degree
  - E021: associate's (was E015 in my first attempt)
  - E022: bachelor's (was E016)
  - E023-E025: graduate (was E017-E019)
  - Fixed by re-running with correct mapping
- **D1 HTTP API loader too slow**: 16 min for 14,450 records. Switched to `wrangler d1 execute --file=` which takes 10s.
- **3 separate queries too slow**: 1500-2100ms for US detail. Combined into 1 query via 3-way LEFT JOIN. Now 600-900ms.
- **Column index formula `2n-1` in 0-indexed Python**: Same as B01001, E{n} at `parts[2*n-1]`. E001 at parts[1], E025 at parts[49].
